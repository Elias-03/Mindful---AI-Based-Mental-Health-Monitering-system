# Quick Start - AI Chatbot 🚀

## You're All Set!

The AI chatbot has been successfully integrated into your mental health journal dashboard.

## Start Using It Now

1. Run the server:
   ```bash
   START.bat
   ```

2. Open your browser to: **http://127.0.0.1:8000/**

3. Log in and you'll see the chatbot at the top of your dashboard!

## What You Get

✅ AI-powered mental wellness companion  
✅ Beautiful chat interface  
✅ Empathetic, supportive responses  
✅ Powered by Google's Gemini AI  
✅ Automatic error handling  

## Important Notes

- **API Quota**: The free tier has rate limits. If you hit the limit, wait 30-60 seconds.
- **Model**: Uses Gemini 2.5 Flash (with automatic fallback to 2.0 Flash)
- **Privacy**: Conversations are sent to Google AI for processing

## Example Questions

- "I'm feeling anxious today"
- "Can you help me relax?"
- "What are some stress management techniques?"
- "I need someone to talk to"
